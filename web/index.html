Point breakdown

This historical score estimates population-level risk; it does not diagnose STEMI, replace clinical judgment, or direct reperfusion or other treatment. Estimates were derived primarily in the fibrinolysis era and may not be calibrated to current care or individual populations.

Calculations run locally in your browser. Entered clinical values are not sent to a server; only static page assets are downloaded.

Source: Morrow DA et al. Circulation. 2000;102:2031–2037. Original publication · TIMI Study Group calculator. This is the original TIMI STEMI score, not the TIMI UA/NSTEMI score.
